## Part 3 — Rules Before Posting in Any Group

1. Read the pinned rules post before anything else
2. Check if there is a "Promo Day" (usually Friday or Sunday) — if yes, save your promotional posts for that thread
3. Spend 10–15 minutes leaving genuine comments on 2–3 existing posts first
4. **Never post the same post in more than one group on the same day** — FB flags this as spam
5. **Never put the link in the post body** — always drop it in the first comment. FB suppresses posts with external links in the body
6. Upload demo videos natively to Facebook (do not share YouTube links) — native video gets 3–5× more reach

## Part 7 — Facebook Algorithm Rules

Follow all of these or your reach tanks.

1. **Link goes in the first comment, never the post body** — FB suppresses reach on posts with external links in the body. Post → then immediately comment with the link yourself.
2. **Upload videos natively to Facebook** — do not post YouTube links. Native video gets 3–5× more organic reach.
3. **Reply to every comment within the first hour** — FB reads conversation volume as quality signal and boosts those posts.
4. **End posts with a micro-action** — "Drop 'link' below", "Comment your time", "React if this hits" — anything that generates comments before the sales click.
5. **Never copy-paste the same post twice in the same day** — even small edits (different first sentence) are enough. Exact duplicates get suppressed.
6. **Best posting times:** Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am or 7–9pm in your target audience's timezone.
7. **Don't delete posts that get zero traction** — editing or deleting tanks account trust. Just move on.
