# ReelForge — Outreach SOP
### Coach & Consultant Prospecting — Full Process

**Who this is for:** Anyone running outreach for ReelForge — founder, VA, or sales rep.
**Goal:** Find coaches/consultants whose Facebook pages have gone quiet, send them a sample video, offer 3 free videos, convert them to a paying customer.
**Time per prospect:** ~10 minutes to research and send the first message.

---

## Overview — The Full Process

```
Step 1 → Find prospects on Facebook
Step 2 → Qualify them (2-minute check)
Step 3 → Send Facebook DM + attach sample video
Step 4 → Follow up on Facebook (Day 4)
Step 5 → Follow up on WhatsApp (Day 6)
Step 6 → Follow up on Email (Day 9)
Step 7 → Close or move on (Day 12)
```

---

## Step 1 — Find Prospects on Facebook

### Where to search

Go to **facebook.com** → search bar → type each query below → filter results to **"Pages"**

### Search queries to use

```
"business coach"
"life coach"
"executive coach"
"leadership coach"
"sales coach"
"mindset coach"
"career coach"
"success coach"
"high performance coach"
"marketing consultant"
"business consultant"
"brand strategist"
"health coach"
"wellness coach"
"relationship coach"
```

Add a city or state after any term to get local results:
```
"business coach Texas"
"life coach New York"
"executive coach London"
"business consultant Florida"
```

### Target countries only

Tier 1 only: **United States, United Kingdom, Australia, Canada.**
Do not spend time on other countries — lower willingness to pay.

## Step 2 — Qualify the Prospect (2-Minute Check)

Before sending anything, open their Facebook Page and check all 4 of these:

| Check | What you're looking for | Pass / Skip |
|-------|------------------------|-------------|
| **Last post date** | 2–4 weeks ago (or longer) | Pass if quiet |
| **Follower count** | 500 – 10,000 followers | Pass |
| **Video content** | Little or no video on their page | Pass — this is the gap |
| **Business type** | Coaching, consulting, advisory | Pass |

**Skip if:** They're posting daily, they already have lots of video, they're a restaurant/salon/retail business (those need real footage — ReelForge can't help them).

### Where to find their WhatsApp and email

- Check their Facebook Page → **About** section
- Check their website (usually linked in the About section)
- Check their Instagram bio if linked
- Check their personal Facebook profile if public

**Record everything in a spreadsheet before moving on.** See the tracker template at the bottom of this doc.

## Step 3 — Send Facebook DM + Attach Sample Video

Send **one** of the messages below. Do not copy-paste the same message to everyone — rotate between them and personalise the first line where possible.

**Attach the sample video directly to the message.** Do not send a link — attach the actual video file. It plays inline in the DM and gets watched.

---

### Message A — For pages that have gone quiet

> Hey [Name] — I was looking through coaching pages in [their niche/area] and noticed yours has been quiet lately.
>
> I made a quick video that would fit your brand — attached it so you can see what I mean.
>
> Would something like this be useful for your page?

---

### Message B — They post but no video

> Hey [Name] — love what you're doing with [mention something specific — their niche, a recent post topic].
>
> I noticed you're not posting much video yet. Made one that matches your style — attached below.
>
> Honest question: is video something you've been meaning to get to?

---

### Message C — Short and direct

> Hey [Name] — I build Facebook video content for coaches and consultants.
>
> Made a sample that fits your brand — check it out (attached).
>
> If you like the direction, I'd love to make you 3 more for free. No strings.
>
> Worth a look?

---

### Message D — Peer/feedback angle

> Hey [Name] — I've been creating Facebook video content for coaches and just finished one I think would actually land well on your page.
>
> Dropping it here — genuinely curious what you think.
>
> [attach video]
>
> If the style works for you, happy to make 2–3 more around your specific niche at no cost. Just want the feedback from someone in the space.

---

### Message E — They post educational/tips content

> Hey [Name] — saw your post on [topic] and thought it'd make a great video.
>
> I made something similar for a coach in [niche] — attached so you can see the format.
>
> Would content like this work for your audience?

---

**Rules for Facebook DMs:**
- Keep it under 5 lines
- Always end with a yes/no question — never end with a statement
- Never use the word "AI" or "automated" in the first message
- Never explain how ReelForge works in the first message — the video does that
- One DM per person. Do not send two messages on the same day.

---

## Step 4 — Facebook Follow-Up (Day 4)

If no reply after 4 days, send this **once**:

> Hey [Name] — just circling back on the video I sent. No pressure at all — just wanted to make sure it didn't get buried.
>
> Still happy to make you 3 free ones if you want to test it on your page.

**After this:** If still no reply, move to WhatsApp on Day 6. Do not send a third Facebook message.

---

## Step 5 — WhatsApp Follow-Up (Day 6)

**Important rules for WhatsApp:**
- WhatsApp is personal — keep it very short (2–3 lines max)
- Do NOT send the video unsolicited on WhatsApp — ask first, wait for a yes, then send
- Only message between 9am–7pm their local time

---

### WhatsApp Message A — Reference the Facebook message

> Hey [Name] — I sent you a message on Facebook a few days ago with a video I made for your coaching page. Not sure if it got buried. Happy to drop it here if easier. Worth a quick look?

---

### WhatsApp Message B — Even shorter

> Hey [Name] — tried Facebook but wanted to make sure this reached you. I made a free video for your page — can I send it here?

---

### WhatsApp Message C — Simple offer

> Hey [Name] — I make Facebook video content for coaches. Made one that fits your brand. 30 seconds to watch — want me to send it?

---

**After they say yes on WhatsApp:** Send the video file + this:

> Here it is — let me know what you think. If the style works, happy to make 2 more for free around your niche. No catch.

**WhatsApp follow-up (Day 9 if no reply to your WhatsApp message):**

> Hey [Name] — no worries if the timing's off. Just wanted to make sure the message came through. Let me know if you ever want to test some video content for your page.

Do not follow up more than once on WhatsApp.

---

## Step 6 — Email Follow-Up (Day 9)

Use one of the subject lines and messages below. Keep it under 120 words. Attach the video or include a link.

### Subject lines (pick one — do not A/B test the same prospect)

```
Made a video for your coaching page
Free video for [Name]'s page — worth a look?
Quick question about your Facebook content
I made something for your brand
```

---

### Email A — Primary email

> Hi [Name],
>
> I reached out on Facebook last week but wanted to make sure this got to you.
>
> I create Facebook video content for coaches and consultants — I made one that fits your brand and wanted to show you what it looks like before saying anything else.
>
> [attach video or paste link]
>
> If the style works, I'd love to make you 2 more for free — no pitch, just want feedback from someone in the space.
>
> Worth a look?
>
> — Mahbub

---

### Email B — Follow-up email (Day 12, if no reply to Email A)

> Hi [Name] — just bumping this up in case it got buried.
>
> Short version: I made a Facebook video that fits your coaching brand and I'm offering 3 free ones to a handful of coaches this month.
>
> Happy to send them over if you're interested — takes nothing on your end.
>
> — Mahbub

---

**After Email B:** If still no reply — stop. Mark them as "No response" in the tracker. Move on.

Do not send more than 2 emails. Do not follow up after Day 12.

## Step 7 — When They Reply

### If they say "yes" / "looks good" / "I'm interested"

Reply immediately:

> Awesome — I'll get started on 3 videos for your brand. Quick question: what topics have been getting the most engagement from your audience lately? Or happy to just pick based on your niche if you'd rather leave it to me.

Then produce the 3 videos using ReelForge and deliver them within the week.

### When you deliver the videos

> Hey [Name] — here are your 3 videos. All made for your brand, reviewed by our team before I sent them over.
>
> [attach videos]
>
> If these work for you, I'd love to keep the momentum going. We have a plan that does 7 videos a week — all produced and reviewed, auto-posted to your Facebook page — for $49/month. Your first week is just $5 to try.
>
> Want me to set you up?

### If they say "not right now" or "maybe later"

> No problem at all — I'll check back in a few weeks. If you ever want to test it, just say the word.

Add them to a "warm" list. Follow up in 3 weeks with a new sample video on a different topic.

### If they ask "how does this work?"

> We analyze your Facebook page and website to build your brand profile — niche, tone, audience. Then we generate a week of video scripts tailored to your brand, produce the videos, and a real person on our team reviews each one before anything posts to your page. You get an email when each video is ready. You can edit or reject anything before it goes live.
>
> It's $5 for a full week (7 videos) to try — cancel anytime.

## What Never to Say

- Never mention "AI," "automated," or "Chrome extension" in outreach
- Never claim videos are instant or delivered in minutes
- Never send more than 2 follow-ups per channel
- Never copy-paste the same message to 10 people in the same group — Facebook flags it
- Never pitch in a Facebook Group — post the sample video as a demonstration, reveal you built the tool in comments only if asked
- Never DM someone who is already posting video content regularly — wrong target
